Usability log

Single-switch scanning is a standard AAC technique. Any on/off signal can drive it: a key or tap, an adaptive USB switch (these commonly act as the Space or Enter key), a blink or jaw-clench sensor, or a BCI command. Here the BCI input is simulated. Speech uses your browser’s voices (Hindi needs a Hindi voice installed; spelling is English only). Hindi phrases should be checked by a native speaker. Not clinically validated: test with real users and speech-language therapists before relying on it. The alert only opens a prefilled WhatsApp message or dialer; it never sends or calls by itself.

Impact 2036 why this matters


The problem
People who lose the ability to move or speak need assistive technology, but access is limited and very unequal.


NEW STROKES IN INDIA / YEAR≈1.25–1.3 million
PEOPLE WHO NEED ASSISTIVE PRODUCTS2.5 billion+
OF THEM DENIED ACCESS≈1 billion
ACCESS IN POOREST COUNTRIESas low as 3%


Sources: Lancet Neurology 2021, via Down To Earth · Lancet SEAR series (GBD 2019), via ThePrint · WHO–UNICEF Global Report on Assistive Technology (2022). These numbers show the scale of the access gap. They are not an estimate of how many people could use a BCI.


Our approach
An open, low-cost BCI research and education platform: it turns EEG-style signals into assistive commands with a safety-first closed loop that adapts to each user.
  • Decode intent from band-power patterns
  • Reject low-confidence predictions instead of guessing
  • Verify, calibrate and retrain per user
  • E-STOP and no connection to real medical hardware


Illustrative user (fictional persona)
“Ravi”, 34, survived a severe stroke and cannot speak or use his hands. A communication board lets him choose YES, NO, HELP, WATER by looking at a tagged tile (SSVEP), and a calibrated decoder can drive a virtual wheelchair.

  • Backend API, SQLite, PDF report, 27 tests
  • SSVEP speller, devices, calibration, E-STOP
  • Real clinical EEG (Bonn dataset): seizure detection about 97%, see Real EEG page
Not yet done (honest)
  • Accuracy on real BCI EEG (motor imagery): script ready, not yet run
  • Real hardware beyond BrainFlow’s synthetic board
  • Any clinical validation or user study


NeuroPilot AI is an educational and research demonstration. It does not read private thoughts, diagnose, treat, or control real medical devices. EEG is sensitive personal data: consent, on-device processing and security come first.
